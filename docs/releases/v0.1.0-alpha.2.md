# v0.1.0-alpha.2

The second alpha of plgrid-llmlab-hpc-burst. It adds Sano as a Provider, including direct dial-home mode there, and the beta features built since alpha.1, opt-in and off by default. Like alpha.1, it's tagged before alpha graduates (HBEP-15, Releases), and the criteria still open are listed below.

Start with [the quickstart](../quickstart.md). From the archive, unpack it, check `SHA256SUMS`, and follow the steps with the archive's `bin/`, `agent-builds/`, `config/crd/`, `profiles/` and `examples/`. No clone is needed.

## Platforms measured

Both rows ran on Sano in direct mode, where each job dials the control-plane host's front itself, from `main` at `0eef3b80`. That commit's shipped code equals this release's (HBEP-15, Releases). The front ran on a development host outside Cyfronet's network.

| `beta-sano-llmlab-baseline` (HBEP-764's Row 1) | an artificial study's load on one Sano Instance with LLMLab behind it: sessions held on their home Instance, the KV cache under load, overflow to LLMLab, and the front's own wait when the Instance is detached | Pass: no spill and no error below the cap; 0 engine preemptions at the long context; above the cap, overflow to LLMLab with 0 errors; detached, the front's queue rose and drained with no 503 |

The per-row posts are on [issue #735](https://github.com/groundnuty/plgrid-llmlab-hpc-burst/issues/735) and [issue #764](https://github.com/groundnuty/plgrid-llmlab-hpc-burst/issues/764). Sano's host-dialed rows (staging, serving and the handover) passed on 18 Nov 2026 on earlier code, and its direct-mode reachability probe (P-D1) on 19 Nov 2026, both on #735. **Athena wasn't run for this release;** alpha.1's Athena rows ran on alpha.1's code.

## Alpha criteria still open

- **On `plgrid-gpu-a100`:** the no-cut handover with two jobs at once (HBEP-5, HBEP-7), and the one-hour tunnel run (HBEP-6), as for alpha.1.
- **On Helios:** every Helios criterion is beta's, to run when the GH200 nodes return (#373).

## The HBEPs

HBEP-1 is `implemented`. HBEP-3 to 11 and 15 are `implementable`, and HBEP-2 and 764 `provisional`. None is at a stage yet: their alpha status PRs follow graduation (HBEP-1).

**New in this release:**
- **Sano** (`profiles/sano.yaml`): its GPU partition with QOS chosen by GPU count and per-job caps, in host-dialed or direct mode (never ssh). A Provider may override the profile's staging directory.
- **Beta features, opt-in and off by default:**
  - HBEP-7's over-allocation: extra Allocations ranked per Provider, hedging, and kept-warm capacity;
  - HBEP-10's agent primitives: the capacity guard and Operations, the `hpcburst` CLI, controller identities with an audit log, start estimates, and `hpcburst inventory`;
  - HBEP-764's Workloads: the `Workload` kind, named per request, pinned to the ModelDeployments that serve it, with a token budget per Instance from the engine's reported KV cache, and LLMLab-first route order with `managedSessions`;
  - beta's one retry to another Instance (HBEP-5, open question 5).

  Each is tested in CI, most on loopback. Workloads also ran on Sano (Row 1 above).
- **Fixes:**
  - vLLM's boolean flags are rendered by the engine image's own parser, from a dump per image (HBEP-3, amended by #788);
  - `status.healthyWorkers` joins router health by worker identity.

## Stated limits that still apply

- **Sano:**
  - **Host-dialed mode** allows one Provider per login host and at most 2 Allocations. Every stack on one host shares the login node's relays, and no stack's controller sees another's.
  - **Direct mode** needs the jobs to reach the control-plane host's dial-home port. `direct` isn't a profile value (HBEP-4), and the reachability is recorded in the profile's facts.
  - **Multi-GPU workers** (tensor or pipeline parallel above 1) need `NCCL_P2P_DISABLE=1` in the engine environment on Sano (docs/operations.md). Without it, NCCL can hang on the 4-GPU node, which has PCIe links only.
  - **The two Sano reference entries** (`expectedPerformance`) were measured at the earlier per-GPU share of 11 CPUs, while this release asks for 4 CPUs and 64 GiB per GPU. They stand until they're next measured.
- **Engines:**
  - The framework is written against vLLM v0.32.0, and other versions run per deployment by image digest.
  - Boolean flags of an image with no recorded dump are rendered by v0.32.0's, with `"false"` refused.
  - An engine environment variable that HBEP-3's `v1` table doesn't list, such as `NCCL_P2P_DISABLE`, is a deployment-ID input.
- **Workloads:**
  - A Workload's charge is its declared shape, not a request's real size.
  - With LLMLab-first order, a conversation may move between LLMLab and our Instances from one turn to the next. Sticky routes (#791) are designed, not in this release.
- **From alpha.1, unchanged:**
  - Short partitions host short tests only.
  - Keep a front at or below 600 tunnels in direct mode and 300 in host-dialed mode (HBEP-6, open question 11).
  - A host-dialed relay's ssh stalls its streams at each rekey (HBEP-6, Risks).
  - The bulk path protects token streams only while the path's bottleneck buffer drains in under about 85 ms (HBEP-6, open question 12).
  - Envoy needs glibc 2.30 or later on the compute node (HBEP-5).
